1. CANONICAL TRANSIT NETWORK & LINE-CODE RECONCILIATION
   - Location: lib/core/transit/canonical_line_table.dart
   - Solves LTA DataMall line code mismatches across feeds:
     * Sengkang LRT: TrainServiceAlerts (STL) <-> PCDRealTime / Forecast (SLRT)
     * Punggol LRT: TrainServiceAlerts (PTL) <-> PCDRealTime / Forecast (PLRT)
     * Circle Extension: TrainServiceAlerts (CCL) <-> PCDRealTime / Forecast (CEL)
     * Changi Branch: TrainServiceAlerts (EWL) <-> PCDRealTime / Forecast (CGL)
   - GroundLevel Enum:
     * Strictly represents official LTA dataset categories: underground & aboveGround.
     * Robust parser normalizes 'at-grade', 'at_grade', 'atgrade', 'aboveground', 'elevated', 'underground', and 'subsurface'.
     * isUnderground helper enables dead-reckoning countdown logic when subway signal is lost.
   - Comprehensive Station Database:
     * Lat/lon coordinates, station codes, line affiliations, exits, and ground level.
     * Rachel's stations (Tampines EW2/DT32, Raffles Place EW14/NS26).
     * Mdm Lim's stations (Bedok EW5, Outram Park EW16/NE3/TE17 for SGH).

3. LTA DATAMALL INTEGRATION SERVICE
   - Location: lib/core/services/lta_service.dart, lib/core/models/
   - TrainServiceAlerts:
     * Parses nested AffectedSegments, direction, station lists, and advisory messages.
     * Extracts real LTA mitigation data (FreeMRTShuttle, FreePublicBus).
   - Station Crowd Density (PCDRealTime & PCDForecast):
     * Real-time 10-minute interval crowding.
     * 30-minute interval forecast for proactive travel warnings.
   - Facilities Maintenance (v2/FacilitiesMaintenance):
     * Station and exit-level lift outage tracking.
   - Bus Arrival & Accessibility (v3/BusArrival):
     * Bus load tracking (SEA = Seats Available, SDA = Standing, LSD = Limited Standing).
     * Feature = WAB (Wheelchair-Accessible Bus identification).
   - Secure Key Injection:
     * Keys passed via --dart-define=LTA_DATAMALL_KEY=... / ONEMAP_KEY=... (never committed to repository).

6. CENTRAL TRANSIT ROUTING & DECISION ENGINE
   - Location: lib/core/services/transit_routing_engine.dart
   - Dynamic Disruption Rerouting:
     * Automatically activates when a rail line disruption is detected.
     * Incorporates real LTA Free MRT Shuttle / Free Public Bus mitigation data as the recommended path.
     * Provides concise one-line explanation of why the route changed.
     * Preserves original delayed route side-by-side with alternative route for visual comparison.
   - Accessibility & Lift Outage Rerouting (Mdm Lim):
     * Automatically detects lift outages at destination station/exit.
     * Reroutes to direct wheelchair-accessible bus (Bus 197 WAB) with seat availability to avoid stairs.
   - Weather-Aware Sheltered Routing (Mdm Lim):
     * Automatically checks 2-hour nowcast for rain.
     * Reroutes walking legs through CoveredLinkWay sheltered network (+2 mins) to keep commuter dry.
   - Confidence Bands:
     * Color-coded ETA confidence rating (Green / Amber / Red).
     * Accompanied by plain-English explanation ("crowd rising + 1 active alert").
